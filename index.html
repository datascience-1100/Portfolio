Quantitative Economics | Econometrics | Applied Research

I am a Master of Science in Quantitative Economics student at the University of Connecticut with a strong interest in empirical economic research, econometrics, and quantitative analysis.

My research uses large-scale survey and microdata, econometric methods, and statistical modeling to examine economically meaningful and policy-relevant questions. My recent work includes studying wage responses to economic recessions and investigating the relationship between financial satisfaction, income, and individual well-being.

Before beginning my graduate studies, I spent more than eight years at the Reserve Bank of India, where my work included banking supervision, regulatory compliance, financial operations, and analysis of financial and regulatory information. This experience continues to shape my interest in applied economic and financial research.
